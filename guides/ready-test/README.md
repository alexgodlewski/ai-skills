# Are you ready? Four questions before you buy an AI setter

Fail one, and don't buy it. Not mine, not anybody's. A free guide by [Alex Godlewski](https://www.instagram.com/alexgodlewsky/).

## 1. Do people write to you every day?

Open your DMs. Count new people in the last 7 days who wrote first or answered you.

**If not:** an AI setter has nothing to answer. Under five new people a day, fix your content or your ads first.

## 2. Do you make $20,000 a month or more?

Look at the last three months, not the best one.

**If not:** fix your offer first. Sell it by hand until people say yes without a discount.

## 3. Does somebody pick up the calls?

Look at last week. How many booked calls happened, and who was on them?

**If not:** a full calendar only hurts you. Find a closer or open your own evenings.

## 4. Do good people wait for an answer?

Take the last 20 good leads. Write down how long each one waited for your first message.

**If not:** you don't need us. If everybody gets an answer in five minutes, day and night, keep your team.

## Four times yes?

Then it makes sense to talk. Reply to my message on Instagram with the word FOUR, and tell me what you sell.

One no? Fix that one first. It costs less than any software.

In ninety days we booked almost ten thousand calls for people like that.
