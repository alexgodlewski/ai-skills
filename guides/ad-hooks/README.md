# 5 hooks for Facebook ads, and the prompt that fills them in

From the BLANKS reel. A free guide by [Alex Godlewski](https://www.instagram.com/alexgodlewsky/).

## The 5 hooks

| # | The hook | Example for a coach |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | If you're a [who]... | If you're a coach trying to get clients from ads. |
| 2 | Most [who] struggle with [problem] because... | Most coaches struggle with ads because they talk to everyone. |
| 3 | The top 3 ways to [result]. | The top 3 ways to get booked calls from ads. |
| 4 | [Who], this one is for you. | Coaches, this one is for you. |
| 5 | The reason [problem] is that [real cause]. | The reason your ads don't work is that they don't speak to one person. |

The one rule: speak to one person. That's where almost everyone gets it wrong.

## The prompt

Paste this into Claude and fill in the three lines at the top.

```text
My offer: [what you sell].
Who it is for: [one type of person, as exact as you can].
The result they want: [the result, in their words].

Fill in these 5 ad hooks for my offer. Write 3 versions of each, in plain words a 12 year old understands.
1. If you're a [who]...
2. Most [who] struggle with [problem] because...
3. The top 3 ways to [result].
4. [Who], this one is for you.
5. The reason [problem] is that [real cause].

Rules: talk to one person. No hype words. No promises I can't keep. Each hook under 15 words.
Then pick the 5 strongest and tell me why each one would stop the scroll.
```

## How to test them

1. **One ad, five first lines.** Keep the same video or picture. Change only the first line.
2. **Give each one a few days.** Look at the cost per lead and the share of leads who can pay.
3. **Keep the winner, write five new ones.** Use the winning hook type and run the prompt again.
