## The map

| Step | What the AI does | What you need |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Trend research | Watches the top reels in your niche every day and pulls the hooks that work right now. | A list of 10 to 20 accounts in your niche and an AI that reads their newest reels. |
| 2. Scripts | One idea goes in, ten scripts come out, in your own words. | Claude, plus 5 of your best captions or scripts so it learns how you talk. |
| 3. Comments | Every comment with your word gets a public reply and a DM in seconds. | A comment automation (Setor flows, or any comment to DM tool). One word per reel. |
| 4. The setter | Answers every DM, day and night, like a real person. | An AI setter trained on your offer, your prices and your tone. |
| 5. The money question | Asks what they make or what they can spend, and lets go of people who can't pay. | One clear question and your minimum, written down. |
| 6. Booking | Finds a time and books the call on your calendar. | Calendly, Cal.com, iClosed, GoHighLevel or TidyCal, connected to the setter. |
| 7. Follow ups | When someone goes quiet, it checks in, so no lead is lost. | 2 to 4 short follow ups, spaced over a few days, that stop when they reply. |
| 8. Tracking | Shows which reel and which automation brought in the money. | Every lead tagged with the reel and the word they came from. |

And you only show up to the calls. The AI books them. People still close them.

## How to start this week

1. **Day 1.** Pick one offer and one word for your next reel. Set up step 3 for that reel only.
2. **Day 2.** Write your money question and your minimum (step 5). Connect your calendar (step 6).
3. **Day 3.** Train the setter on your offer (step 4) and test it as if you were a new follower.
4. **Day 4.** Add the follow ups (step 7).
5. **Day 7.** Look at step 8: which reel brought comments, calls and money. Make more of that one.

## Good to know

1. **Start with one reel.** The map only works if one link works first.
2. **The AI books, people close.** Do not let it promise prices or results you would not promise on a call.
3. **Read the first 20 conversations.** Fix what it gets wrong in its training, not in each chat.
