## People come in two ways

They comment under a post. Or they follow you from a post and write later.

Views show you neither. You need the post next to the person, and then the call and the money next to the post.

## Way 1 comments

1. **One word for one reel.** Never use the same word under two reels. The word tells you the reel.
2. **One automation for one word.** Name it like the reel. The name stays with the person.
3. **Say: comment the word.** Not: write to me. If they just write, the post is lost.
4. **Ask for the whole word.** Set it to match the full word, so a normal comment does not start it.

## Way 2 new followers

1. **Open your notifications.** Look for: started following you from your reel.
2. **That line names the reel.** Instagram shows it for most new followers, not for all.
3. **Read it while the reel is new.** Most followers of a reel come in the first days, and old lines move down fast.
4. **Put the reel next to the person.** In your CRM or in their chat notes, the same day.

## Then add calls and cash

1. **Booked a call.** Write the date next to the person. Count it for the reel they came from first.
2. **Paid.** Write how much. One sale belongs to one reel.
3. **Both ways count.** A reel can bring people who comment and people who follow. Add them up.

## Every Monday

1. **Sort your reels by calls, not views.** Look at the top three.
2. **Make the first one again.** Same idea, new example, new first line.
3. **Zero calls after two weeks?** Do not make five more like it, even if it has the most views.
4. **A dash is not a zero.** If nobody read the notifications when the reel was new, you don't know.
