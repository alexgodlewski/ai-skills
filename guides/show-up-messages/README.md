## A no show is not a maybe

618 people booked a sales call and never showed up. 37 of them booked again. 11 came. 6 bought.

So the work is before the call, not after it. These are the three messages.

## Message 1: right after they book

Send it in the first five minutes.

> You're booked: [day], [time], your time. You told me you want [the thing they said]. On the call we look at that, and you leave with [one thing they get]. Reply YES so I know you saw this.

They read their own words, and they say yes a second time. If nobody says YES in a few hours, ask: does the time still work?

## Message 2: the morning of the call

If the call is today, skip this one.

> Today at [time]. I read what you wrote me about [their problem]. Bring one thing: [a number, a screenshot or a question]. If today is bad, tell me now and I'll give you a new time.

Bringing one thing makes the call theirs. The easy way out saves you an empty hour.

## Message 3: one hour before

One link, nothing else to read.

> One hour to go. Here's the link: [link]. It takes [length] minutes. A quiet place is enough. See you at [time].

Send it where they talk to you. If they write in DMs, send it in DMs.

## One more: if they still don't come

Send it in the first ten minutes.

> I'm on the call now. Is everything OK? If it was a bad moment, I have [time today] or [time tomorrow]. Which one?

Out of a hundred people who don't show up, ninety four never book again. This is your one good try.

## And fix the calendar

1. **Close next week.** Call inside 24 hours: 76 out of 100 show up. Three to five days away: 63.
2. **Open today and tomorrow.** Two times are enough. One of them in the evening.
3. **Booked for today?** Send message 1 and message 3.
4. **Booked for tomorrow or later?** Send all three.
