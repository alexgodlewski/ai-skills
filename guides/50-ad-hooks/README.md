# 50 ad hooks for coaches

The first line of your ad decides if anyone watches the rest. Here are 50, in the five types from the HOOKS reel, written for coaches. Swap the words in [brackets] for your own. A free list by [Alex Godlewski](https://www.instagram.com/alexgodlewsky/).

## 1. Call out the right person

Name who it is for and the problem. The right people stop, everyone else scrolls.

1. If you're a coach struggling to get clients, this is why.
2. If you're a coach with an empty calendar, this is for you.
3. If you post every day and still get no sales calls, this is why.
4. If you're a fitness coach stuck at [number] clients, watch this.
5. If you're a business coach who hates sales calls, this is for you.
6. If your DMs are full but your bank account isn't, this is why.
7. If you're a coach paying for ads that bring the wrong people, this is for you.
8. If you're a new coach with no audience yet, this is for you.
9. If people say your offer is "too expensive", this is why.
10. If you're a [type of] coach and your leads go quiet after one message, watch this.

## 2. The number one mistake (then tell a story)

Name the mistake, then start a story and hold back the answer. They keep watching to find out.

11. Here's the number one mistake keeping coaches from a full calendar.
12. Here's the number one mistake keeping coaches from clients who can pay.
13. The biggest mistake I see coaches make in their DMs.
14. The one mistake that made my first year of coaching so hard.
15. Here's the mistake keeping your ads from bringing in calls.
16. Most coaches make this mistake in the first message they send.
17. The mistake that cost me months of empty calls.
18. Here's why your best leads never book a call.
19. I made this mistake for a year before a client told me.
20. Here's the number one mistake keeping [who] from [result].

## 3. Did you know

Point out a problem people don't know they have. This works well with cold traffic. Only use a number if you can prove it.

21. Did you know most coaches answer new DMs too late?
22. Did you know most leads never get a second message?
23. Did you know most people who comment on your post never hear from you?
24. Did you know the people who need you most often don't know it yet?
25. Did you know most coaches sell to people who can't pay?
26. Did you know a lot of your sales calls are with the wrong people?
27. Did you know the first reply to a lead matters more than the post?
28. Did you know most coaches give up on a lead after one try?
29. Did you know your best clients may already follow you?
30. Did you know most [who] have this problem and never notice it?

## 4. Your own story

Start with who you are and what you did. It feels like a real post, not an ad. Only say what is true for you.

31. I'm a coach who works from home, and here's how I got my calendar full.
32. I'm a [age] year old coach, and here's how I got my first [number] clients.
33. I'm a mom of two, and here's how I run my coaching business in 4 hours a day.
34. I was a personal trainer at a gym. Here's how I went online.
35. I stopped doing sales calls with everyone. Here's what happened.
36. I'm a coach with a small audience. Here's how I still sell every week.
37. I used to answer every DM myself. Here's what I do now.
38. I quit my job to coach full time. Here's the first thing I'd do again.
39. I'm a coach who hates selling. Here's how I get clients anyway.
40. I'm a [who], and here's how I got [result] in [time].

## 5. I'm looking for five

Ask for a small number of very specific people. It is one of the oldest hooks out there, and it still works.

41. I'm looking for five coaches who want more clients without more posting.
42. I'm looking for five coaches who want a full calendar without cold DMs.
43. I'm looking for five fitness coaches who want online clients without the gym.
44. I'm looking for five business coaches who want sales calls without running ads.
45. I'm looking for five coaches who want to stop chasing leads.
46. I'm looking for five new coaches who want their first paying client this month.
47. I'm looking for five coaches who want to sell a higher priced offer.
48. I'm looking for five coaches who want leads that are ready to pay.
49. I'm looking for five coaches who want their DMs answered while they sleep.
50. I'm looking for five [who] who want [result] without [pain].

## Make them fit your business

Paste this into Claude.

```text
I'm a coach. Who I help: [one type of person]. What they want: [result]. What they hate: [pain].

Here are 50 ad hooks in 5 types. Rewrite the 10 that fit my business best, in plain words a 12 year old understands.
Rules: talk to one person. No numbers or results I can't prove. Each hook under 15 words.
Then tell me which 3 to test first and why.

[paste the list]
```

## How to test them

1. **Keep the ad the same, change only the first line.** Then you know the hook did it.
2. **Test 3 to 5 hooks at a time.** Look at the cost per lead and how many leads can pay.
3. **Keep the winner, try the next type.** One good hook type often works across many ads.
