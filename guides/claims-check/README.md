# The claims check

Read your ads and your page with this list tonight. A free guide by [Alex Godlewski](https://www.instagram.com/alexgodlewsky/).

## What happened

A company that sells an online course agreed to pay $1.5 million. The US government said its ads promised income most buyers never made. The order is final since July 2, 2026.

Each new break of an order like this can cost up to $53,088.

This list is not legal advice. It shows you what to fix before you ask a lawyer.

## Check 1 numbers

- [ ] Every number has a name, a date and a file next to it.
- [ ] You had the proof before you posted the number.
- [ ] Income numbers say how many people made it, out of how many.
- [ ] No number about what they will make. Only what somebody made.
- [ ] Views are not called clients. Booked is not called paid.

No proof? Take the number out tonight.

## Check 2 screenshots

- [ ] Next to your best client there is what a normal client gets.
- [ ] You know the normal result. If you don't, the screenshot is not a promise.
- [ ] How long it took is on the same screen.
- [ ] The screenshot is not cut in a way that changes it.
- [ ] The client said yes to showing it.

Small letters saying results are not typical do not fix it. The government tested that.

## Check 3 reviews

- [ ] If somebody got something for it (money, a prize, a free month), it says so next to the review.
- [ ] If it is from your team, your family or a partner, it says so.
- [ ] Nobody had to write a good one to get a refund or a gift.
- [ ] No bought reviews, no made up people, no bought followers.
- [ ] You don't hide the bad ones and call the rest all reviews.

Say it next to the review, not at the bottom of the page.

## Check 4 refunds

- [ ] The rules are next to the word guarantee, not behind a link.
- [ ] The rules now are the rules they bought with.
- [ ] Somebody who asks gets an answer in a few days.
- [ ] Nobody has to post something good to get their money.

The course company had to fix this one too.

## Say this instead

1. **Make $10,000 a month.** Anna made [number] in [months]. Most clients get [normal result].
2. **Works for everyone.** Works for [who], when they do [what].
3. **I get 50 clients a month.** We booked [number] calls in [month]. [number] of them bought.
4. **Guaranteed.** If [this] doesn't happen by [day], you get [what]. Here are the rules.

## Look in every place

- [ ] Ads that run now, and old ads you can turn on again
- [ ] Your bio and pinned posts
- [ ] The sales page and the page after it
- [ ] Emails, the old ones too
- [ ] What your setters copy and paste in DMs
- [ ] Slides and recordings of your calls and webinars
