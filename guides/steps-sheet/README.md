## From an ad to a sale

24,490 people followed seven businesses because of an ad. 9,617 answered the first message. 767 booked a call. 327 showed up. 123 bought.

Your numbers will be different. What matters is the step where you lose the most people.

Open steps-sheet.xlsx, fill in the five green boxes, and it tells you.

## Fill in five numbers

1. **New followers from your ads** Ads Manager, last 30 days.
2. **Answered your first message** Count the chats where they wrote back.
3. **Booked a call** Count people, not calls.
4. **Showed up** Only calls that happened.
5. **Bought** Only people who paid.

## What each step tells you

1. **Few answer?** Make the first message shorter. Eight words or fewer did better on most accounts we checked.
2. **They answer, but don't book?** This is the part in the middle. Answer their question first, ask one thing, offer two times.
3. **They book, but don't come?** The call is too far away. Open today and tomorrow.
4. **They come, but don't buy?** Wrong people or a weak call. Listen to three calls before you change the ad.

## About our numbers

1. **They are from seven businesses.** Ads for new followers, then messages, then a call.
2. **Showed up and bought are the lowest they can be.** We count only calls where the team wrote down what happened.
3. **So for showing up look at 71.** On accounts that write it down, 71 out of 100 booked calls happen.
4. **Change one step at a time.** Two weeks, one change, then look again.
