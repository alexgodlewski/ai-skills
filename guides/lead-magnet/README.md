# The lead magnet so good it scares people

Two Claude prompts and one message, from the MAGNET reel. A free guide by [Alex Godlewski](https://www.instagram.com/alexgodlewsky/).

## How it works

1. **Find the pain.** Claude researches what your ideal client complains about on Reddit.
2. **Turn it into your offer.** Claude finds the deepest pains, in their own words.
3. **Send the free video.** You offer a short video that fixes that exact problem, in the words they used.

Claude can only research Reddit if it can open web pages. In the Claude app, turn on web search. For deeper research, connect a tool that really opens pages (see my free guide Real research: https://github.com/alexgodlewski/ai-skills/tree/main/guides/real-research).

## Prompt 1 find the pain on Reddit

```text
Research what [IDEAL CLIENT] complain about on Reddit when they try to [WHAT THEY WANT, e.g. get more clients].
My ideal client: [WHO THEY ARE, e.g. online coaches who make under $10k a month].
Return four lists:
1. Keywords: 10 to 15 phrases they type when they look for help.
2. Pain phrases: 10 to 15 sentences they write about this problem, in their own words.
3. Subreddits: 5 to 10 communities where they hang out.
4. Free guide words: phrases that show they want a free guide, template or checklist.
Keep every list short. No intro, no summary.
```

## Prompt 2 turn the pain into your offer

```text
Use the research above. Find the 5 deepest pains my ideal client feels right now, in their own words.
For each pain give me:
- Who feels it: the type of person.
- How bad it is: a number from 1 to 10.
- The exact words they use: 2 short quotes from the research.
- One sharp question: a single question they cannot ignore.
Put the strongest pain first. Only use pains that show up in the research.
```

## Step 3 the message

Send this in the DM, with the problem in the exact words they used:

```text
I made a quick video on how to fix [their exact problem] for you. Can I send it over?
```

Record the video only when they say yes. Keep it under 3 minutes. Show the fix, then ask one question.

## Good to know

1. **Check the quotes.** Ask Claude for the links to the threads it quotes, and open two or three yourself.
2. **One pain, one magnet.** Pick the strongest pain only. A lead magnet for everything works for nobody.
3. **Use their words in your ads too.** The pain phrases make good first lines.
