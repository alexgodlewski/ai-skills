## The one page

1. **How many people wrote to you this week and got only one message back?**  
   38 out of 100 of our calls come after a follow up.  
   Fix first: three follow ups, then stop.
2. **How many calls are booked more than three days away?**  
   Inside 24 hours 76 out of 100 show up. Three to five days away, 63.  
   Fix first: open today and tomorrow, close next week.
3. **How many no shows got a message in the first hour?**  
   Out of 100 who don't come, 94 never book again.  
   Fix first: one message in ten minutes, with two new times.

## Send this to your setter

Today, by name. Ask for numbers, not for a story.

> Please send me three numbers for the last 7 days by [day]: 1. how many people wrote to us and got only one message back, 2. how many calls are booked more than three days away, 3. how many no shows got a message in the first hour. If you can't see one of them, tell me which one.

If they can't tell you, stop right there. That is the first thing to fix, and it costs you nothing.

## Then decide

1. **One of the three is bad?** Fix it for two weeks. A second person pays for the same hole twice.
2. **All three are good, and people still wait?** Count how many chats wait more than one hour for a first answer. That is a real reason to hire.
3. **Nobody writes to you?** You don't need a setter. You need people.
4. **Before you sign anybody:** Write down the three numbers. Look at them again after 30 days. They should move.
