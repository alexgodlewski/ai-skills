## The one you never sent

We looked at 3,571 booked calls. 1,365 of them came after a follow up. That is 38 out of a hundred. 16 out of a hundred needed two or more.

Most people who go quiet did not say no. They got busy.

Open your DMs. Find everybody who got one message and then nothing. Send them these, in this order.

## Follow up 1: the next day

Say back the thing they told you.

> You said you want [their words]. Is that still true?

Example: You said you want your evenings back. Is that still true? Use their words, not yours.

## Follow up 2: on day three

Give them one thing. Then one small question.

> I thought about what you told me about [their problem]. One thing to try: [one sentence]. Did you try that before?

One sentence of real help. No link, no file, no voice note of four minutes.

## Follow up 3: on day seven

Make it easy to say no.

> Should I leave this for now? If yes, tell me and I won't write again. If not, what stopped you?

It is easy to answer, and a clear no is good too. Then stop.

## Four rules

1. **One question in each message.** Two questions get no answer.
2. **Never write: just checking in.** It gives them nothing to answer.
3. **Write in the evening.** People write most at nine at night.
4. **Stop after three.** If they wrote no, stop after that one.

## If a tool sends it

1. **Instagram has a 24 hour rule.** A tool may write only in the 24 hours after their last message. Follow up 1 has to fit in it.
2. **By hand there is no such rule.** So follow ups 2 and 3 are for a person.
3. **Only people who wrote to you.** Never send these to a list of strangers.
