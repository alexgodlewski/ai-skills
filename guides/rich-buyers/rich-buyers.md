## Five changes for your ads

Two changes are settings in Meta. One is a filter. Two are about how you look and what you write.

Meta's own pages say value rules can make the cost per result go up. You pay more for the people you want. So change one thing at a time and count calls, not leads.

Changes 4 and 5 are my own rules of thumb. I have no study for them. Test them.

## Change 1 bid down weak places

1. **What a value rule is.** It tells Meta to bid more or less for a group inside one ad set. It is not an off switch. The most you can lower a bid is 90 percent.
2. **Where it is.** In your ad set, look under the performance goal for Value rules or Rule set. Names change. If you do not see it, it is not open for your campaign type.
3. **Make the rule.** Choose Placement. Pick Facebook Reels. Choose decrease. Type 90. Add a second rule for Audience Network, also 90.
4. **Why Reels and Audience Network.** Only if they bring weak leads for you. Check your leads by placement first.

## About value rules

1. **Limits.** Up to 10 rules in a rule set. An increase can be 1 to 1,000 percent, a decrease 1 to 90.
2. **Order matters.** If a person fits two rules, only the first one counts.
3. **Not for every campaign.** Meta's developer page says it does not work for Leads campaigns that send people to your website or to a phone call. It worked for some Sales and App campaigns. It changed several times. Look in your own ad set.
4. **The cost goes up.** Meta says the overall cost per result may rise.

## Change 2 test it by device

1. **This is a test, not a rule.** Do not turn it on because of a feeling.
2. **First look.** Break your leads down by device. Which phones bring people outside your market? Look at 100 leads at least.
3. **Then test.** Copy the ad set. In the copy, add a rule: Operating system, Android, decrease 50. Leave the first one alone.
4. **Wait.** Two weeks. Compare price per booked call, not price per lead.
5. **Keep it only if** the copy gets more calls for the money.

## Change 3 let only good ones in

1. **Why.** Meta looks for more people like the ones you tell it are wins. If you call every form a win, it finds more of everyone.
2. **How.** Ask one question on your form. Send good people to a calendar page with the pixel. Send the rest to a page with no calendar and no pixel.
3. **The full steps.** They are in the free guide The 2 door setup. It has a prompt that builds both pages for you.

## Change 4 look expensive

1. **Clean background.** No clutter, no messy room, no bright mix of colours behind you.
2. **Good light.** Face a window, or use a lamp in front of you. Not light from behind.
3. **Clear sound.** Bad sound looks cheaper than bad video.
4. **Only what is true.** No fake office, no rented car. Look like the best day of your real business.

## Change 5 change your words

1. **Time, not money.** Not: Save $2,000 on your taxes. Try: Get your Fridays back.
2. **Done for you, not do it yourself.** Not: Learn how to run your own ads. Try: We run your ads. You take the calls.
3. **Protect, not grow.** Not: Make more. Try: Keep what you built safe.
4. **Want, not need.** Not: You need help. Try: Here is the business you want.

## Let Claude rewrite it

Paste your ad into the first bracket. Fill the other three. Paste it into Claude.

```text
Rewrite my ad for buyers who have money. Here is the ad:

[paste your ad: the main text, the headline and the button]

What I sell: [one sentence]
Who it is for: [one sentence]
What is true and can be proven: [facts, numbers and results you can show. Nothing else.]

Rewrite it with these four changes:
1. TIME over money. Talk about the hours and weeks they get back, not about a low price or saving money.
2. DONE FOR YOU over do it yourself. Say what we do for them so they do not have to.
3. PROTECT over grow. Talk about keeping what they built safe, and about the trouble they avoid, before you talk about getting more.
4. WANT over need. Write about the life or the business they want, not about what is wrong with them.

Rules:
- Plain words. Short sentences. A 12 year old must understand it.
- No dashes. No hype words. No promise of money or results. No numbers I did not give you.
- Do not say or hint that I think they are rich. Never write "rich" or "wealthy" in the ad.
- If one of the four changes does not fit my offer, say so and skip it. Do not lie to make it fit.
- Keep the offer and the button the same.

Show the result as a table with two columns, "Old" and "New", one row for each part (headline, first line, main text, button). Under the table list what you changed and why, one line each. Then give me 2 more versions of the headline to test.
```

## Before you post it

1. **Check the claims.** No income promises. Use the free guide The claims check on the new text.
2. **One test at a time.** Run the new words in a copy of the ad, not in place of the old one.
3. **Count calls.** After two weeks, which ad made more booked calls for the money?
4. **Keep the old one.** If it still wins, it wins.
