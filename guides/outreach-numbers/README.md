# The Outreach Numbers Sheet

A free tracker by [Alex Godlewski](https://www.instagram.com/alexgodlewsky/). It tracks every number in your outreach, from the first message to the closed deal. It also tells you what to fix when a number looks wrong.

**Files**

- [outreach-numbers.xlsx](outreach-numbers.xlsx): Excel file with three tabs (works in Excel, Numbers and Google Sheets)
- [outreach-numbers.csv](outreach-numbers.csv): the same weekly tracker as plain text

## How to use it

1. Open the **Weekly tracker** tab.
2. Rows 2 and 3 hold example numbers so you can see how it works. Delete them and type your own.
3. Once a week, add one row. Fill in the white columns (B to J). The green columns (K to O) fill themselves.
4. Look at the rates. Find the first one that looks bad, going left to right. Then open the **What to fix** tab.

## The numbers

| Number | What it is | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|
| People contacted | How many new people you messaged | Your volume |
| Replies | People who wrote back | Whether the right people see and like your message |
| Not interested | Replies that said no | Whether you reach the wrong people, or the offer is unclear |
| Follow ups sent | Later messages to people who went quiet | Whether you do the follow up work at all |
| Replies to follow ups | Replies that came after a follow up | Whether your follow ups work |
| Calls booked | Calls on the calendar | Whether interest turns into a next step |
| Calls taken | Calls that really happened | Whether people show up |
| Deals closed | People who said yes and paid | The result |
| Speed to lead | Minutes from their first message to your first reply | Whether warm people stay warm |

## The rates (they calculate for you)

- **Reply rate** = replies / people contacted
- **Booking rate** = calls booked / replies
- **Show rate** = calls taken / calls booked
- **Close rate** = deals closed / calls taken
- **Follow up reply rate** = replies to follow ups / follow ups sent

## How to read it (what to fix)

- **Many people contacted, almost no replies:** check who you contact, how your messages are delivered, and the message itself.
- **Replies, but no bookings:** the interest is there. Fix your follow ups, make the next step tiny, offer two exact times.
- **Calls, but no sales:** find where confidence drops on the call. Is it the pitch, the process or the price? Fix one at a time.
- **Slow speed to lead:** reply in minutes, not hours.

The **What to fix** tab has the full list with what to check for each.

Tip: fix the first number that looks wrong, from left to right. There is no point working on your pitch if nobody replies.

## Import into Google Sheets

**Option A: the Excel file (best)**

1. Download [outreach-numbers.xlsx](outreach-numbers.xlsx) from this folder (open the file on GitHub and click the download button).
2. Go to [sheets.google.com](https://sheets.google.com) and open a blank sheet.
3. Click **File**, then **Import**, then **Upload**, and pick the file.
4. Choose **Replace spreadsheet** (or **Insert new sheet(s)**) and click **Import data**. All three tabs come in, with the formulas.

**Option B: the CSV file**

1. Download [outreach-numbers.csv](outreach-numbers.csv).
2. In a blank Google Sheet, click **File**, then **Import**, then **Upload**.
3. Choose **Replace current sheet** and click **Import data**.
4. The CSV gives you the weekly tracker only. The formulas are in the rate columns (K to O). If they show as text, click a cell and press Enter, or re-type the formula from the row above.
