# Test Your Sales IQ: 16 Sales Moves, Each With the DM to Send

A free guide by [Alex Godlewski](https://www.instagram.com/alexgodlewsky/). Sixteen sales moves in four levels. For each one: what it means, the exact DM to send, when to use it, why it works, and what to watch out for.

**PDF (18 pages):** [sales-iq-16-cards.pdf](sales-iq-16-cards.pdf)

The prices and plans in the DMs are examples. Use your real offer, your real prices, and only promises you keep.

## Level 1

### Upsell

Offer a bigger version of what they want.

> Want the 12-week plan? It adds a weekly call.

**Use it when:** right after they say yes.  
**Why it works:** They already said yes, so a better version is an easy next step.  
**Watch out:** Upselling before they said yes to the first thing.

### Cross-Sell

Offer something that goes with it.

> Want the meal plan too? It goes with the program.

**Use it when:** after the first yes.  
**Why it works:** It solves the next problem they will have anyway.  
**Watch out:** Offering something that does not fit what they bought.

### Bundling

Put a few things together for one price.

> Program, meal plan and check-ins: $497 for all three.

**Use it when:** they want more than one thing.  
**Why it works:** One price is simpler than three choices.  
**Watch out:** Putting things in the bundle nobody asked for.

### Follow-Up

Message again when they go quiet.

> Hey, did you get a chance to look at the plan?

**Use it when:** 2 days of silence.  
**Why it works:** Most quiet leads are busy, not gone.  
**Watch out:** "Just checking in." Ask one clear question instead.

## Level 2

### Down-Sell

Offer a smaller version instead of losing the sale.

> If $2,000 is too much right now, the 4-week plan is $500.

**Use it when:** price is the only problem.  
**Why it works:** A small yes keeps them as a client.  
**Watch out:** Cutting your price. Cut the offer, not the price.

### Qualifying

Check if they are a good fit.

> What are you trying to fix, and by when?

**Use it when:** before you send a link.  
**Why it works:** You spend your time only on people you can really help.  
**Watch out:** Sending your link before you know if they fit.

### Risk Reversal

Take the risk off them.

> Do the work for 30 days. No result, full refund.

**Use it when:** only if you really honor it.  
**Why it works:** Fear of losing money is often the last thing in the way.  
**Watch out:** Promising a refund you would not really give.

### Foot In The Door

Ask for a small yes first.

> Can I send you a 2-minute video first?

**Use it when:** before you ask for a call.  
**Why it works:** People who said a small yes find the next yes easier.  
**Watch out:** Asking for the call in your very first message.

## Level 3

### Assumptive Close

Talk like they already said yes.

> Does Monday or Wednesday work for your first call?

**Use it when:** they are clearly in.  
**Why it works:** Picking a day is easier than deciding to buy.  
**Watch out:** Using it on someone who still has questions.

### Trial Close

Check how ready they are before the ask.

> From 1 to 10, how close is this to what you need?

**Use it when:** before you send the price.  
**Why it works:** The number tells you what is still missing.  
**Watch out:** Sending the price to someone who just said 4.

### Objection Pre-Handling

Answer the doubt before they say it.

> Most people ask if this works with a full-time job. It does.

**Use it when:** you know the usual doubt.  
**Why it works:** A doubt you answer first never turns into a no.  
**Watch out:** Raising doubts they never had.

### Discovery

Questions that make them say what they need.

> What have you tried so far, and why didn't it work?

**Use it when:** early in the chat.  
**Why it works:** Their own words tell you what to offer and how to say it.  
**Watch out:** Pitching before you know what they need.

## Level 4

### Takeaway Close

Pull the offer back a little.

> Honestly, this isn't for you if you can't give it 3 hours a week.

**Use it when:** they are unsure, never as a trick.  
**Why it works:** People lean in when they feel something could slip away.  
**Watch out:** Faking it. Only say it if it is true.

### Door In The Face

Big ask first, then the real, smaller one.

> The full program is $3,000. Or start with the $500 sprint.

**Use it when:** only with a real bigger offer.  
**Why it works:** The real offer feels small next to the big one.  
**Watch out:** Making up a big offer you do not really sell.

### Price, Then Silence

Say the price. Then stop typing.

> It's $1,500.

**Use it when:** let them answer first.  
**Why it works:** Whatever they say next is the real objection.  
**Watch out:** Adding a discount before they even answer.

### The Nibble

One small extra after the yes.

> Done! Want the onboarding call too? It's $100.

**Use it when:** right after they pay.  
**Why it works:** Right after paying, people are most open to a small add.  
**Watch out:** Big add ons. Keep it small and useful.

## How to use this

1. Pick ONE move this week. Not all sixteen.
2. Copy its DM and change the words until it sounds like you.
3. Send it in 5 real chats. Write down what people answer.
4. Keep it if it works. Then go to the next move.
