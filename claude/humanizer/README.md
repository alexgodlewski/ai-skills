# humanizer

**Claude can now rewrite your text so it does not sound like AI.**

It finds the patterns that give AI away: the "not this, it's that" line, the long dash, the word "quietly", purple prose, lists of three, "here's the thing", fake questions, hype words and more. Then it rewrites your text in your own voice, using a few things you wrote yourself.

**[Download humanizer-skill.md](https://github.com/alexgodlewski/ai-skills/releases/latest/download/humanizer-skill.md)**

## Install (30 seconds)

1. Open the Claude desktop app and click the **Code** tab.
2. Drag `humanizer-skill.md` in and type **set this up**.
3. Paste your text and say: *Humanize this.* Give it 2 or 3 things you wrote yourself, so it can match your voice.

It makes writing read like a person wrote it. It cannot promise that an AI detector will pass it. The source is right here: [`SKILL.md`](SKILL.md).
