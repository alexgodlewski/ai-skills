# agents

**Exactly what to build with 100 Claude agents, for your business.**

Tell Claude what your business does. It proposes the builds that fit you and writes the exact Claude Code prompt for each one, with the `ultracode` word and a team size. It also has 10 ready examples for business owners: research 100 competitor sites, enrich a lead list, rewrite a whole website, audit every ad, and more. It tells you the truth about cost and time before you start.

**[Download agents-skill.md](https://github.com/alexgodlewski/ai-skills/releases/latest/download/agents-skill.md)**

## Install (30 seconds)

1. Open the Claude desktop app and click the **Code** tab.
2. Drag `agents-skill.md` in and type **set this up**.
3. Type `/agents`, or say: *What should I build with a team of agents?* Claude asks a few questions about your business and gives you the prompts.

Big agent teams use a lot of Claude usage. The skill tells you to test with 5 first. The source is right here: [`SKILL.md`](SKILL.md).
