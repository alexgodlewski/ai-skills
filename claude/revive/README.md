# revive (for Claude)

**Don't send "just following up". Paste the chat where a lead went quiet, and Claude writes the next message that gets a reply.**

Most leads do not say no. They go quiet. Type `/revive` and paste the chat, and Claude does the work:

- **Finds the stage:** after the first DM, after a price or link, before the call, after a no-show, or after "I need to think about it".
- **Guesses why they went quiet,** using what is in your chat.
- **Writes 2 or 3 messages in your voice,** each with a different angle, and tells you when to send each.
- **Gives a short follow-up plan:** how many touches, how far apart, and when to stop.
- **Stays respectful:** only in the channel where the lead already talks to you, with no guilt and no pressure.

**[Download revive-skill.md](https://github.com/alexgodlewski/ai-skills/releases/latest/download/revive-skill.md)**

## Install in Claude Code (30 seconds)

1. Open the Claude desktop app and click the **Code** tab.
2. Drag `revive-skill.md` in and type **set this up**.
3. Type `/revive` and paste the chat, for example: `/revive` then the DM thread with a lead who saw my price and went quiet 4 days ago.

## Install in claude.ai (browser)

Open `SKILL.md` below, copy everything under the second `---` line, and paste it into a new Project's instructions at claude.ai. Then start a chat in that Project and paste your conversation. It works the same way.
