# lead-finder

**Claude can now scroll social media for you and find you customers.**

Type `/lead-finder` and paste your website. Claude works out who you sell to, finds people who talk about that problem in public on Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, X and Reddit, and writes down each person's pains, dreams and objections in their own words. It finds their public contact details and writes the outreach from the words they used. Four parts in one skill: a scraper, a profile builder, an email finder and a sender.

**[Download lead-finder-skill.md](https://github.com/alexgodlewski/ai-skills/releases/latest/download/lead-finder-skill.md)**

## Install (30 seconds)

1. Open the Claude desktop app and click the **Code** tab.
2. Drag `lead-finder-skill.md` in and type **set this up**.
3. Type `/lead-finder yourwebsite.com`.

You bring your own free [Apify](https://apify.com) account and token (`APIFY_TOKEN`). Scraping costs Apify credit, and Claude tells you the estimate and tests with 10 results first. Outreach is saved as **drafts** in your Gmail through the Gmail connector: you read them and press send yourself. Claude cannot send DMs for you either, so it writes them for you to paste.

Honest limits: many people have no public email, platforms have rules and can block scrapers, and nothing here promises replies. The full list is in [`SKILL.md`](SKILL.md).
