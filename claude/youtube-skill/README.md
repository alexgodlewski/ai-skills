# youtube-skill

**Claude can now watch YouTube videos for you and learn any skill.**

Give Claude a YouTube video, a whole playlist or channel, or a podcast. It reads all of it, learns what it teaches, and saves it as a skill it keeps and uses from then on. Drop in the best sales training on YouTube and Claude writes your follow-ups that way. Drop in a creator's hook videos and it writes your hooks that way.

**[Download youtube-skill.md](https://github.com/alexgodlewski/ai-skills/releases/latest/download/youtube-skill.md)**

## Install (30 seconds)

1. Open the Claude desktop app and click the **Code** tab.
2. Drag `youtube-skill.md` in and type **set this up**.
3. Paste a link and say: *Watch this, learn what it teaches, and turn it into a skill I can use forever.*

It reads captions first and falls back to a free speech model on your own computer for videos without captions and for podcasts. The source is right here: [`SKILL.md`](SKILL.md) and [`transcript.py`](transcript.py).
