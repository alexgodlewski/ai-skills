# call-prep (for Claude)

**Answer your lead's questions before the sales call, so more of them show up ready to buy.**

When someone books a call, they wait. In that gap they doubt the price, search your name, and wonder if it works for them. This skill plans everything they see between booking and the call. Type `/call-prep` with your offer, and Claude:

- **Builds a call prep map:** a table with each doubt, the content that answers it, where the lead sees it (a DM with a link, a Story, a retargeting ad or an email), and how many days before the call.
- **Uses what you already have:** it matches your Reels, testimonials, case studies and FAQ to each doubt first.
- **Writes the 3 to 5 missing scripts:** short, filmable on your phone, with a slot for your real proof.
- **Writes the DM messages** that deliver each piece, plus the reminder and the reply for a lead who goes quiet.
- **Sets up retargeting in plain words** if you run ads. You choose the budget. It never makes up numbers.

No fake urgency and no tricks around ad rules.

## Install in Claude Code (30 seconds)

1. Open the Claude desktop app and click the **Code** tab.
2. Drag `call-prep-skill.md` in and type **set this up**.
3. Type `/call-prep` and your offer, for example: `/call-prep a $3,000 coaching program for new gym owners, calls booked from Instagram DMs`.

## Install in claude.ai (browser)

Open `SKILL.md` below, copy everything under the second `---` line, and paste it into a new Project's instructions at claude.ai. Then start a chat in that Project and describe your offer. It works the same way.
