# skill-thief

**Get the best prompt anyone has shared for a job, rebuilt as your own skill.**

Type `/skill-thief` and tell Claude the job you need done: research my competitors, answer my customer emails, write my quotes. It searches the public places where people share prompts and skills (GitHub, Anthropic's own skills and docs, Reddit, public prompt libraries), picks 5 to 10, scores them against your job and tests the best two on a real example of yours.

Then it does not just copy. It writes a new skill in its own words with your business in it, saves it, and links every source. From then on Claude does that job the same way every time.

**[Download skill-thief.md](https://github.com/alexgodlewski/ai-skills/releases/latest/download/skill-thief.md)**

## Install (30 seconds)

1. Open the Claude desktop app and click the **Code** tab.
2. Drag `skill-thief.md` in and type **set this up**.
3. Type `/skill-thief`. Claude asks for the job, a few lines about your business and one real example.

## What you need

- Claude with a way to search the web. If it has none, you paste in links to prompts you like and it works from those.
- Nothing to pay, no extra account.

## Limits

- It finds only what is public and what Claude can reach. Some sites block automated readers.
- It never copies a private or paid prompt. If it sees no license, it learns the idea and writes new words.
- A test on one example is a small test. Use the new skill on five real jobs and tell Claude what to change.
