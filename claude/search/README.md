# search (for Claude)

**Get your business named in Google's AI answer.**

Google now writes an AI answer at the top of the page, and most people never scroll past it. If that answer names your competitor and not you, you just lost the customer. Type `/search` and your website into Claude. It sends agents to check how Google's AI sees your business, finds what is missing (reviews, service pages, the answers to the questions your customers actually ask), and then fixes it.

No one can force an AI answer. This makes you the easiest business for AI to trust and name.

**[Download search-skill.md](https://github.com/alexgodlewski/ai-skills/releases/latest/download/search-skill.md)**

## Install (30 seconds)

1. Open the Claude desktop app and click the **Code** tab.
2. Drag `search-skill.md` in and type **set this up**.
3. Type `/search yourbusiness.com`.

In Claude Code it sends 12 agents at the same time: six search the questions your customers type, six check one area each (reviews, service pages, customer questions, business details, proof, page structure). If your website's files are open in Claude Code, it makes the changes after you say yes. If not, it writes every fix ready to paste.

Using Claude in the browser instead? Download [`search-claude.zip`](https://github.com/alexgodlewski/ai-skills/releases/latest/download/search-claude.zip) and upload it under Settings, Capabilities, Skills. It does the same checks one after another.
