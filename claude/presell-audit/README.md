# presell-audit (for Claude)

**Find out why your content gets views but not buyers.**

Pre-selling means people are mostly sold before they see your offer. Your content does that job when it does three things. Paste in your last posts, scripts or captions, and this skill checks every one for all three:

- **One single desire.** Does the content make your offer the best option right now, and show why the other paths are worse?
- **Frustrations.** Does it name what your buyer already tried that failed?
- **Beliefs.** Does it break the beliefs that stop them from buying or acting?

For each post you get a score out of 5 on each of the three, the weak lines quoted exactly, and a rewrite of those lines in your own voice. At the end you get your weakest area, the pattern across your content, and **5 new post ideas that pre-sell**, each with a hook, an outline, and the real proof you should bring.

Made for coaches and service businesses.

**[Download presell-audit-skill.md](https://github.com/alexgodlewski/ai-skills/releases/latest/download/presell-audit-skill.md)**

## Install (30 seconds)

1. Open the Claude desktop app and click the **Code** tab.
2. Drag `presell-audit-skill.md` in and type **set this up**.
3. Type `/presell-audit` and paste in your last 5 to 20 posts or scripts, plus a few lines about your offer.

## What you need

- No account and no API key. Paste your posts in, or drop in a text file or a caption export.
- A few lines about your offer: what you sell, who it is for, the result, and how people buy.
- If you give a profile link, Claude tries to read your recent posts with a browser tool. If it cannot see them, it says so and asks you to paste them. It never guesses what a post said.

## What it cannot do

- The scores are a careful reading by an AI against a fixed rubric. They are not sales data. The real test is whether people reply, book or buy.
- It never makes up client results or testimonials. Where proof is needed, it leaves a bracket for you to fill in with something real.
- With fewer than 5 posts it still works, but the sample is small and it tells you so.
