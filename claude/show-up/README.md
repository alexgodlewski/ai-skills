# show-up (for Claude)

**Get more of your booked calls to actually happen.**

Half the people who book a call never show up. This skill builds the messages that go out between the booking and the call, so showing up is the easy choice. Type `/show-up` and your offer, and Claude gives you:

- **A reminder matrix:** which message goes out when, based on how far ahead the call was booked (same day, next day, or 3 or more days out).
- **Every message written in full:** right after booking, the day before, the morning of, 1 hour before and 5 minutes before, in your voice if you paste a sample of your writing.
- **Short messages with one job each:** every one gives a reason to show up and an easy way to reschedule instead of ghosting.
- **Email, Instagram DM and SMS:** SMS only for people who agreed to texts.
- **Setup steps** for calendar app reminders and email tool automations, in plain words.

It never invents results or reviews. If you have no proof to share, it leaves that out.

**[Download show-up-skill.md](https://github.com/alexgodlewski/ai-skills/releases/latest/download/show-up-skill.md)**

## Install in Claude Code (30 seconds)

1. Open the Claude desktop app and click the **Code** tab.
2. Drag `show-up-skill.md` in and type **set this up**.
3. Type `/show-up` and your offer, for example: `/show-up a 30 minute strategy call for fitness coaches, calls booked 2 to 4 days ahead, I use email and Instagram DM`.

## Install in claude.ai (browser)

Open `SKILL.md` below, copy everything under the second `---` line, and paste it into a new Project's instructions at claude.ai. Then start a chat in that Project and describe your offer. It works the same way.
