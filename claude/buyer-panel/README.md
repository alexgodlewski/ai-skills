# buyer-panel (for Claude)

**Test your idea on 20 simulated buyers before you spend a dollar.**

Most people find out an offer does not sell after they pay for it. This skill finds out first. Type `/buyer-panel` and your offer, and Claude runs three parts:

- **The Board:** 3 advisors who poke holes in your plan.
- **The CFO:** works out your real margin per sale and break-even from the numbers you give. It asks for the ones you forgot and never makes them up.
- **The Buyer Panel:** 20 simulated buyers who fit your target customer, each with a name, income, habits, current situation and doubts. Each one says buy or not buy, and why in one sentence. Then you get the tally and the top 3 objections.

The buyers are simulated, so they find weak spots, they do not prove demand. The skill tells you to test the winner on real people next.

**[Download buyer-panel-skill.md](https://github.com/alexgodlewski/ai-skills/releases/latest/download/buyer-panel-skill.md)**

## Install in Claude Code (30 seconds)

1. Open the Claude desktop app and click the **Code** tab.
2. Drag `buyer-panel-skill.md` in and type **set this up**.
3. Type `/buyer-panel` and your offer, for example: `/buyer-panel a $500 website audit for dentists`.

## Install in claude.ai (browser)

Open `SKILL.md` below, copy everything under the second `---` line, and paste it into a new Project's instructions at claude.ai. Then start a chat in that Project and describe your offer. It works the same way.
