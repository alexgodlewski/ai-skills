# lead-heat (for Claude)

**Some leads are ready to buy, some are just looking. Paste the DM chat and Claude tells you which, and what to send next.**

Type `/lead-heat`, give it your offer, price and ideal client once, then paste your DM chats. For each lead, Claude:

- **Scores Heat and Fit** with a clear table: pain in their own words, urgency, budget signs, who decides, how well they know you, and red flags.
- **Sorts every lead** into hot, warm, cold or wrong fit, and quotes the line that decided it.
- **Writes the next message** in your voice: a booking message for hot leads, one question for warm leads, one helpful nudge for cold leads, and a kind no for wrong fits.
- **Writes a 5-line pre-call brief** for your closer when a lead has booked.

**[Download lead-heat-skill.md](https://github.com/alexgodlewski/ai-skills/releases/latest/download/lead-heat-skill.md)**

## Install in Claude Code (30 seconds)

1. Open the Claude desktop app and click the **Code** tab.
2. Drag `lead-heat-skill.md` in and type **set this up**.
3. Type `/lead-heat` and your offer, for example: `/lead-heat a $3,000 12-week coaching program for new gym owners`. Then paste your DM chats.

## Install in claude.ai (browser)

Open `SKILL.md` below, copy everything under the second `---` line, and paste it into a new Project's instructions at claude.ai. Then start a chat in that Project and describe your offer. It works the same way.
