# outcome-first

**Stop writing prompts about how. Describe the result.**

OpenAI's own prompting guide says it: start with the result, not a detailed list of steps. The more you tell the AI how to do something, the worse it does. This skill makes ChatGPT ask you the right questions about the finished result first (who it is for, what done looks like, what it must include, what it must avoid), writes a one-page result brief, and builds the whole thing in one go once you say yes.

**[Download outcome-first.zip](https://github.com/alexgodlewski/ai-skills/releases/latest/download/outcome-first.zip)**

## Install

1. In ChatGPT, open **Skills**, click **Create**, then **Upload from your computer**, and pick the zip.
2. No Skills on your plan yet? Create a Project and paste the text of [`SKILL.md`](SKILL.md) (everything under the second `---` line) into its instructions.

## Try it

> Build me a landing page for my coaching offer.

ChatGPT asks a few short questions about the result, shows you the brief, and builds it after your yes.

Works in Claude too: upload the same zip under Settings, Capabilities, Skills.
