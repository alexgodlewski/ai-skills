# google-ads

**Turn ChatGPT into a Google Ads machine.**

Paste your website and this skill gives you three tools:

- **Competitor ad finder:** looks up the search ads your competitors run right now in the Google Ads Transparency Center (their headlines, offers and landing pages), and finds the gap nobody covers.
- **Ad writer:** writes responsive search ads (15 headlines, 4 descriptions, every character limit checked) based on what already works.
- **Keyword tool:** builds ad groups by intent, keywords and a negative keyword list.

Drop in a report exported from your own Google Ads account and it also tells you what works, what wastes money and where to move the budget.

**[Download google-ads.zip](https://github.com/alexgodlewski/ai-skills/releases/latest/download/google-ads.zip)**

## Install

1. In ChatGPT, open **Skills**, click **Create**, then **Upload from your computer**, and pick the zip.
2. No Skills on your plan yet? Create a Project and paste the text of [`SKILL.md`](SKILL.md) (everything under the second `---` line) into its instructions. Turn on web search, or agent mode to browse the Transparency Center.
