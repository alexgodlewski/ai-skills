# AI Search Fix

Google now puts an AI answer at the top of many searches, and most people never scroll past it. If that answer names a competitor, the business just lost the customer. AI answers name the businesses they can find clear proof about. This skill checks that proof and writes what is missing.

Be honest with the person: nobody can force an AI answer. This makes the business the easiest one for AI to trust and name.

## 1. Get the basics

From the website the person gives you (open it with web search or browsing), work out:

- the business name, what it sells, and its main services;
- the town or area it serves;
- the questions a real customer would type, for example "best plumber in [town]", "emergency plumber near me open now", "how much does [service] cost in [town]".

If something is unclear, ask one short question, then continue.

## 2. See who AI names today

Search the web for 5 to 8 of those customer questions. For each one, note which businesses are named or linked at the top, and whether this business shows up. Do not invent results: if you cannot search, say so and ask the person to paste what they see.

## 3. Check the proof, area by area

Check the website and public listings for each of these, and mark each one **OK**, **Weak** or **Missing**:

| Area | What AI looks for |
|---|---|
| Reviews | Recent reviews on Google and other sites, with words that match the services |
| Service pages | One clear page per main service, saying what it is, where, and for whom |
| Customer questions | Plain answers to the questions people actually ask (prices, timing, areas, guarantees) |
| Business facts | Same name, address, phone and hours everywhere (site, Google Business Profile, directories) |
| Proof | Photos of real work, licences, years in business, case examples |
| Structure | A page title and first paragraph that say the service and the town; schema markup (LocalBusiness, FAQPage) |

## 4. Write the fixes

For every Missing or Weak area, write the actual fix, ready to paste:

- **Reviews:** a short, friendly message the business can send to happy customers asking for a review, mentioning the service.
- **Service pages:** a draft page for each missing service (title, first paragraph with service and town, what is included, area served, price guide if they share it, three questions and answers).
- **Customer questions:** an FAQ section with 6 to 10 real questions and plain answers.
- **Business facts:** a list of exactly where the details do not match.
- **Structure:** the JSON-LD schema code for LocalBusiness and FAQPage, filled in.

## 5. Finish with a short report

A table of the areas with OK, Weak or Missing, then the three fixes that matter most, in order. Keep it under one screen before the drafts.

## Rules

- Never make up reviews, prices, licences or facts. Leave a clear [fill in] where you do not know.
- Never promise a ranking or a place in AI answers.
- Plain words. No jargon unless you explain it in the same sentence.
