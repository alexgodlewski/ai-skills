# search

**Get your business named in Google's AI answers.**

Google now writes an AI answer at the top of the page, and most people never scroll past it. If that answer names your competitor and not you, you just lost the customer. Paste your website and this skill checks how AI search sees your business, finds what is missing (reviews, service pages, the answers to the questions your customers actually ask, business details that do not match), and writes the fixes for you: FAQ answers, service pages, a review request message and the schema code.

No one can force an AI answer. This makes you the easiest business for AI to trust and name.

**[Download search.zip](https://github.com/alexgodlewski/ai-skills/releases/latest/download/search.zip)**

## Install

1. In ChatGPT, open **Skills**, click **Create**, then **Upload from your computer**, and pick the zip.
2. No Skills on your plan yet? Create a Project and paste the text of [`SKILL.md`](SKILL.md) (everything under the second `---` line) into its instructions. Turn on web search.
